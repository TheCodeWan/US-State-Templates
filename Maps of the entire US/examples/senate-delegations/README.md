# U.S. Senate delegations before and after the 2026 midterms

Matching maps use the same geography, colors, and 4076 × 3888 PNG resolution.

- [After-midterms PNG](us-states-senate-delegations.png) · [Editable HTML](us-states-senate-delegations.html)
- [Before-midterms PNG](history/us-states-senate-delegations-before-2026-midterms.png) · [Editable HTML](history/us-states-senate-delegations-before-2026-midterms.html)

The after map shows the incoming Senate based on calls available November 9, 2026. The before map shows the election-eve composition of the 119th Congress. Results are unofficial until certified.

| Color | Meaning | Before: states | After: states |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Red | Both Senate seats Republican | 25 | 17 |
| Blue | Both seats in the Democratic caucus | 22 | 23 |
| Purple | One Republican and one Democratic-caucus seat | 3 | 8 |
| Red/purple diagonal stripes | One Republican seat; the other race unresolved | 0 | 2 |

Independents Angus King (Maine) and Bernie Sanders (Vermont) count with Democrats because they caucus with them. D.C. is gray because it has no voting U.S. senators and is excluded from state totals. Stripes show alternative possible delegation colors, not vote shares or a probability estimate.

## State classifications

| Category | Before midterms | After midterms |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Blue | AZ, CA, CO, CT, DE, GA, HI, IL, MA, MD, MI, MN, NH, NJ, NM, NV, NY, OR, RI, VA, VT, WA | Same states, plus ME |
| Red | AK, AL, AR, FL, IA, ID, IN, KS, KY, LA, MO, MS, MT, NC, ND, NE, OH, OK, SC, SD, TN, TX, UT, WV, WY | AL, AR, FL, ID, IN, KY, LA, MO, MT, ND, NE, OK, SD, TN, UT, WV, WY |
| Purple | ME, PA, WI | IA, KS, NC, OH, PA, SC, TX, WI |
| Striped | None | AK, MS |

The old Senate has 47 Democratic-caucus seats and 53 Republican seats. The incoming map accounts for 54 Democratic-caucus seats, 44 Republican seats, and two uncalled seats. Maine turns blue; Iowa, Kansas, North Carolina, Ohio, South Carolina, and Texas turn purple. Alaska and Mississippi remain unresolved and could be red or purple.

The incoming colors assume same-party replacements for Minnesota and Tennessee senators leaving for their governorships. Future appointments or affiliation changes may require an update.
